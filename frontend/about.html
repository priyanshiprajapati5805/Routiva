WHO WE ARE

About Routiva

Routiva is a smart ride-sharing platform designed to make daily transportation affordable and convenient. We connect drivers who have available seats with passengers travelling on similar routes. Our platform helps people share travel costs, find regular rides and manage their journeys from one place.

OUR STORY

How Routiva Started

Daily transportation can be expensive and finding a regular ride is not always easy. Many people travel on similar routes but do not have a simple way to share their journeys. This inspired the idea of Routiva – a platform where drivers and passengers can connect and travel together while reducing transportation costs.

OUR MISSION

Making Daily Travel Affordable

Our mission is to provide a simple and accessible ride-sharing experience that helps people save money, find convenient rides and manage their everyday transportation.
